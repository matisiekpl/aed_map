## Nature of the app

The app is for information purposes. It shows the locations of AED defibrillators recorded in OpenStreetMap. It does not replace a call to the emergency number (112 in Europe, 911 in the US) or the instructions of a medical dispatcher. In a life-threatening situation, help must be called first.

The service provider does not guarantee that a listed defibrillator exists, is in working order or is accessible during the stated hours, or that its location is accurate. The route calculated in the app is approximate.

Editing requires an OpenStreetMap account and is subject to the [OpenStreetMap Contributor Terms](https://wiki.osmfoundation.org/wiki/Licence/Contributor_Terms). Changes should describe real, verified defibrillators. Changes are saved to OpenStreetMap under the username and are public.

A photo should show a defibrillator or its surroundings and must not contain images of people, personal data or unlawful content.
